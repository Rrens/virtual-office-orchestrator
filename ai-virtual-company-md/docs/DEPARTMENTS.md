# Departments

## Executive

Owns company-level objectives and coordination.

Roles:

- Orchestrator
- Business Strategist

## Product

Turns business objectives into product requirements.

Roles:

- Product Manager
- Business Analyst
- UX Researcher
- Product Analyst

## Design

Defines product experience and visual language.

Roles:

- UI/UX Designer
- Design System Designer
- Brand Designer

## Engineering

Builds and operates software.

Roles:

- Backend Engineer
- Frontend Engineer
- Mobile Engineer
- QA Engineer
- Security Engineer
- Penetration Tester
- Performance Engineer
- DevOps/SRE
- AI Engineer

## Growth

Acquires and retains users.

Roles:

- Digital Marketer
- SEO Specialist
- Content Creator
- Growth Analyst

## Sales

Converts qualified opportunities into customers.

Roles:

- Sales Representative
- Sales Researcher
- Account Manager

## Customer

Handles customer questions, incidents, and feedback.

Roles:

- Customer Service
- Customer Success

## Data

Turns operational data into insight and reliable pipelines.

Roles:

- Data Analyst
- Data Engineer

## Operations

Runs internal company processes.

Roles:

- Operations Manager
- HR Agent
